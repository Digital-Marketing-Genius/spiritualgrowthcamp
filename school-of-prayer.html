Prayer can be learnt.

The disciples watched Jesus pray, then asked Him one thing: "Lord, teach us to pray." That request tells us prayer is an art, and it can be learnt.

But prayer is not learnt by theory alone. In learning to pray, you must pray. The School of Prayer combines a quick daily teaching with a one-hour prayer session every day for 40 days.

By the end, you will not only know more about prayer. You will have built an altar that keeps burning.

What you will learn

Praying the Scriptures
