Gifts, power and partnership.

The Holy Spirit is not an idea to study from a distance. He is a Person to know, to follow and to partner with.

The School of the Spirit is a physical camp meeting where we learn about the gifts, power and partnership of the Holy Spirit, with practical demonstrations of His power.

It is held from city to city, and churches and ministries are invited to partner with us to host one in their city.

Questions

Where is the next camp?

Camps are held city to city as partners invite us. Hosting requests help us plan where to go next.

Who can host?

A church, ministry or fellowship with a local team and access to a suitable venue. Send a request and we will talk it through with you.

What does a host provide?

Typically a venue, a local team and help reaching people in your city. We discuss the details together before anything is confirmed.

Can I attend without hosting?

Yes. Follow our updates or send a request to say you would like a camp in your city.
