About the author

Tayo Alofun

Tayo Alofun is the founder and lead instructor of Mustard Seed, a Christian community that helps believers grow in sound doctrine through devotionals, Bible studies, podcasts, prayer meetings and other faith-based resources.

With a heart for discipleship and a passion for making spiritual truths accessible, he combines biblical insight with real-life application to help everyday Christians live out their faith.
