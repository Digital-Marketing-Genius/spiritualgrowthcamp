Why three years

Finish the Bible without burning out.

Most one-year plans ask for three or four chapters a day, and it is easy to fall behind. One chapter a day is a pace you can keep, with time left to understand what you read.

Questions

Good to know.

How long does it take to read the whole Bible?

The Bible has 1,189 chapters. At one chapter a day you finish in about three years (a little over three years and three months). You can always read ahead on days you have more time.

What is free?

The first seven days, Genesis 1 to 7, are completely free with the full experience: text, audio, commentary and keynote. No card is needed to start.

How much is the membership?

$12 USD a year, which works out to $1 a month. It is billed once a year and renews automatically until you cancel.

Which Bible translation do you use?

The Berean Standard Bible (BSB), a clear, modern and faithful translation that is free to read.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing is lost. Your progress is saved, and the app simply opens at your next unread chapter.

Is there a mobile app?

Through the Bible works in the browser on any phone or computer today. iPhone and Android apps are coming, and your account and progress will move with you.

How do I cancel?

Manage or cancel your membership from your profile in the app, or email hello@spiritualgrowthcamp.com.
