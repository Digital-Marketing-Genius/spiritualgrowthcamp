Questions

What happens after I apply?

Your application and payment are confirmed by email straight away. We review every application personally and email you a decision before the cohort begins.

What if I am not accepted?

Your $50 is refunded in full to the card or account you paid with. Refunds usually reach you within 5 to 10 business days.

How much time does it take?

Plan for about one hour a day for devotion, reading and journaling, plus the bi-weekly activation meetings and a monthly one-on-one session.

Can I pay in my local currency?

Yes. Checkout shows the price in your local currency where available, and accepts cards and other popular payment methods.
